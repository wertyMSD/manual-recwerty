# Recording

## Before recording

Configure these options on the home panel:

- **Microphone**: Select your input device or disable it.
- **System sound**: Enable or disable system audio capture.
- **Custom intro**: Enable to include an animated intro with your brand.
- **Motion effects**: Clicks, cursor trail, particles.

## During recording

- A **system tray icon** appears indicating you are recording.
- **Alt+R**: Stop recording.
- **Alt+Z**: Toggle manual zoom.
- The timer overlay shows elapsed time.

## After recording

The **Post-Recording** dialog appears:

1. **Preview**: Play back the recorded video.
2. **Background options**: Choose from:
    - **Auto**: Background automatically generated from the video's color.
    - **Preset (HD)**: Premium predefined backgrounds.
    - **None**: No additional background.
3. **Keep or discard**: Choose whether to keep the recording or discard it.

!!! tip "Post-processing"
    If you choose to keep it, the video processor opens where you can adjust effects before the final render. See [Processing](../procesamiento/index.md) for details.
