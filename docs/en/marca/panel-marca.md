# Brand panel

1. **Logo**: Upload your company logo (PNG, JPG). RecWERTY automatically extracts the dominant colors.
2. **Company name**: The name that will appear in the intro.
3. **Colors**: Primary, secondary, and accent. They are auto-detected from the logo or you can adjust them manually.
4. **Typography**: Select the font for text.
5. **Intro template**: Choose from:
    - **Logo Reveal**: 3s cinematic intro with glow.
    - **Minimal Fade**: 1.5s elegant fade.

## Branding dialog

Accessible from the Brand panel. Includes:

- Animated intro preview.
- Manual color adjustment with pickers.
- Drag-and-drop logo upload.
- "The End" configuration with logo, email, and phone.

!!! tip "Auto colors"
    When uploading a logo, RecWERTY automatically extracts the dominant colors, but you can adjust them manually if preferred.
