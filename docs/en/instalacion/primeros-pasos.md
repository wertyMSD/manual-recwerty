# Getting started

1. Open RecWERTY. It opens in full screen with a modern dark interface.
2. In the left sidebar, select **Home**.
3. Choose your **capture mode**: Full screen, Window, or Custom region.
4. Configure audio, branding, and effects options from the side panels.
5. Click **START RECORDING**.

!!! success "You're recording"
    You are now recording. See the [Recording](../captura/grabacion.md) section for all capture options.
