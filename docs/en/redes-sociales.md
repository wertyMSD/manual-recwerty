# Social media export

RecWERTY includes optimized presets for social platforms.

## Available presets

| Platform | Resolution | FPS | Bitrate | Max duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | 1920×1080 | 60 | 12 Mbps | Unlimited |
| **TikTok** | 1080×1920 | 30 | 6 Mbps | 3 min |
| **Instagram Reel** | 1080×1920 | 30 | 6 Mbps | 90 sec |
| **Twitter/X** | 1280×720 | 30 | 5 Mbps | 2 min 20 sec |
| **LinkedIn** | 1280×720 | 30 | 5 Mbps | 10 min |
| **WhatsApp** | 720×1280 | 30 | 2 Mbps | 16 min |

## How to use

1. Set up the recording with the **Social** preset.
2. Or use the manual preset from export settings.
3. When processing, select the target platform.

!!! tip "Max duration"
    Respect each platform's maximum duration to avoid clips or upload rejections.
