# RecWERTY user manual for Windows screen recording

> **RecWERTY**: Professional screen recording with motion effects and audio enhancement.
> Version 3.2.3 | Windows

---

## What is RecWERTY?

RecWERTY is a professional screen recording tool designed to create **software demos** and **tutorial videos** quickly, with no editing required. Record your screen and get a polished video with visual effects, audio enhancement, and branding in one step.

### Key capabilities

- **Screen recording**: Full screen, specific windows, or custom regions.
- **Real-time visual effects**: Click ripples, cursor trail, particles, and transitions.
- **Keyboard sounds**: Polyphonic sounds that follow your typing, with key-type variation.
- **Audio enhancement**: Studio-grade voice filters, noise reduction, compression, and equalization.
- **Branding**: Animated intros with logo, corporate colors, and typography.
- **Optimized export**: Presets for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, and more.
- **GIF export**: Optimized high-quality GIFs.
- **Render queue**: Process multiple jobs in parallel.
