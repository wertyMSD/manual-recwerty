# GIF export

You can export segments of your videos as optimized animated GIFs.

## GIF presets

| Preset | Max width | FPS | Colors | Dithering |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **High quality** | 800px | 15 | 256 | Floyd-Steinberg |
| **Balanced** | 600px | 10 | 128 | Bayer |
| **Fast** | 400px | 8 | 64 | None |

## Usage

Select the GIF export option from the post-recording dialog or from the video library.

!!! tip "Quality vs size"
    - **High quality**: Ideal for web GIFs, with better dithering and more colors.
    - **Balanced**: Good quality-size ratio for general use.
    - **Fast**: Small files, quick to generate, ideal for previews.
